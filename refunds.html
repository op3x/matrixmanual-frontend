Refunds and cancellation

ManualOS is operated by Op3x LLC. This page explains how plans are billed, how to cancel, and how refunds work. If anything here is unclear, email support@manualos.com.

01Plans and prices

  • Starter: $39, paid once, plus applicable taxes, includes 3 manuals, and is not a subscription.
  • Pro: $79 per month, plus applicable taxes, with unlimited manuals while your plan is active.

Prices are in USD, plus applicable taxes.

Op3x LLC is the seller of ManualOS. Op3x LLC processes payments and refunds for these purchases and handles payment disputes.

Link is the reseller and the merchant of record. Link processes payments and refunds, and Link handles payment disputes. Link may still refund a purchase within its own refund window. If Link refunds a purchase or accepts a dispute, that purchase is treated as refunded and the credits and access from it end.

02Cancelling Pro

Cancelling Pro. Pro renews automatically every month until you cancel. You can cancel online, self-serve, from our Manage plan link. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period: you keep Pro until then and won't be charged again. We'll email you a confirmation.

You can manage or cancel your subscription online at the reseller's order-management site, app.link.com. That is a full online alternative.

Email backup. If you cannot use an online option, email support@manualos.com and we will cancel it for you.

Starter doesn't need to be cancelled, because it is a one-time purchase.

03Refunds

A purchase can be refunded until its first manual is delivered, within these limits:

  • Starter: you can get a full refund within 30 days of purchase, as long as no manual from that purchase has been delivered.
  • Pro: each monthly charge can be refunded until that billing period ends, as long as no manual has been delivered in that period. Ask before your plan renews.

Once a manual is delivered, that purchase or charge can't be refunded, except where required by law. To ask for a refund, email support@manualos.com from the email address you used at checkout. If a run fails with an error, we give the credit back; if anything goes wrong on our side, email support and we'll restore it. If a payment or a generated manual is wrong, email support@manualos.com and we'll help. Your finished PDF is stored for up to 24 hours so you can download it, then deleted. Save your copy.

Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show the credit.

Changes apply from your first renewal at least 14 days after we email you about them.

  • Chargebacks and payment disputes: access is suspended while a dispute is open, restored if it's decided in ManualOS's favor, and permanently revoked for the disputed purchase if ManualOS loses it. A dispute doesn't stop you from buying from us again, but we may decline a purchase if we reasonably suspect fraud or abuse.
