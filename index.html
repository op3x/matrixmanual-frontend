03 How it works

From file to manual in three steps

No documentation department required. You bring the program. ManualOS writes the explanation.

  1. 01

    Upload a PLC export

    After checkout, upload an L5X, L5K, SCL, TcPOU, PLCopen XML, or tag list, or a STEP, IGES, or DXF model.

  2. 02

    ManualOS writes the manual

    It reads the routines, tags, and comments in the file and drafts a plain-English explanation of what the equipment does.

  3. 03

    Download the PDF

    You get a manual you can print, hand to a technician, or leave with the machine.

08 Questions

Questions

Which files can I upload?

ManualOS accepts L5X and L5K XML, PLCopen XML, IEC 61131-3 structured text (.st, .scl, and .exp), .TcPOU objects, CSV and XLSX tag lists, and STEP, IGES, and DXF models. An ACD file is accepted, but the manual is limited.

What is in a manual?

You get a PDF operating and maintenance manual. The 60-page sample on this page is the SAMPLE-A Case Packer from Example Machinery Co. (fictional), document OM-SAMPLE-A-EN. It includes safety, machine description and theory of operation, transport, installation, commissioning, controls, operation, alarms and troubleshooting, maintenance, spare parts, and appendices. Where a sentence is tied back to the PLC program, the manual shows a code reference note.

What counts as one manual?

Each time ManualOS generates a manual from a PLC file, that counts as one manual. Starter is a one-time $39 purchase and includes 3 manuals. Pro is $79 per month and includes unlimited manuals while the subscription is active.

Who can see my PLC program?

Your file is sent to ManualOS only so it can write the manual. Programs are not sold, and they are not used to train models. Files are processed for your manual and then discarded.

Can I cancel Pro, and what about refunds?

Pro is month to month. You can cancel anytime by emailing hello@manualos.com, and you will not be billed again after the current period. Starter is not a subscription. It is a one-time $39 purchase for 3 manuals. If a payment or a generated manual is wrong, email hello@manualos.com and we will help.
