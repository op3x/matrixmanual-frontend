02 Accepted today

Supported formats

PLC: .L5X, .L5K, .scl, .st, .exp, .awl, .acd, PLCopen .xml, and .TcPOU. Tag lists: .csv and .xlsx. A tag-table XML is tags only, not the program. CAD: .step and .stp, .iges and .igs, .stl, and ASCII .dxf. Archives: a .zip of those files.

Accepted file formats
Ref Format Files Notes
01 PLC program export .L5X .L5K .ACD XML export, including ladder. A manual from .ACD alone is limited.
02 Structured text .SCL .ST .EXP .AWL IEC 61131-3
03 PLC object .TcPOU
04 PLCopen XML .XML Program export
05 Tag list .CSV .XLSX A tag-table XML is tags only, not the program.
06 CAD .STEP .STP .IGES .IGS .DXF .STL DXF is ASCII. STEP, IGES, and STL are the 3D model. DXF is a 2D drawing.
07 Zip .ZIP A zip of the files above

03 How it works

From file to manual in three steps

You upload the file. ManualOS writes the manual.

  1. 01

    Upload the file

    After checkout, upload a file from the supported list, or a .zip of those files.

  2. 02

    ManualOS writes the manual

    It reads the routines, tags, and comments in the file and drafts a plain-English explanation of what the equipment does.

  3. 03

    Download the PDF

    You get a manual you can print, hand to a technician, or leave with the machine.

06 Who it is for

Who it is for

  • Small manufacturers

    You have a machine and the files for it, and no one on staff whose job is writing manuals.

  • Maintenance techs

    You need the equipment explained in plain language while you are at the machine.

  • Integrators

    You are handing a cell to a customer and need a manual that matches the files you shipped.

Privacy

To write your manual, we send content extracted from your files, such as program logic, comments, and part details, to a third-party service provider that helps write the manual. We delete your uploaded files when the job finishes. Download your PDF right away. You can re-download it for 24 hours after it's made, and then it's deleted. Our provider deletes what we send it within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or comply with the law. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

What is in a manual?

You get a PDF operating and maintenance manual. The 60-page sample on this page is the SAMPLE-A Case Packer from Example Machinery Co. (fictional), document OM-SAMPLE-A-EN. It includes safety, machine description and theory of operation, transport, installation, commissioning, controls, operation, alarms and troubleshooting, maintenance, spare parts, and appendices. Where a sentence is tied back to the PLC program, the manual shows a code reference note.

What counts as one manual?

One generation run is one manual: one upload of up to 25 files or a .zip, whether those files are PLC, CAD, or both. If we can't read your files, or the run ends in an error, it doesn't use a credit.
