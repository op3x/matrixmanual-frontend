01The service

ManualOS reads PLC program files and CAD files and writes a plain-English equipment manual as a PDF. Supported formats:

  • PLC program files: .L5X, .L5K, .scl, .st, .exp, .awl, .acd, PLCopen .xml, and .TcPOU
  • Tag lists: .csv and .xlsx. A tag-table XML gives tags only, not the program.
  • CAD files: .step and .stp, .iges and .igs, .stl, and ASCII .dxf
  • Archives: a .zip of those files

Limits:

  • Each file can be up to 50 MB, and you can upload up to 25 files at a time.
  • A manual made from a native .ACD file alone is incomplete and will say so.
  • Other formats are not accepted. We may add or change supported formats over time.
